# 1. Project Identity

## Project Name

**Personal IR Workspace for Peach**

Operational workspace and lightweight information ecosystem developed to support:

- International Relations operations
- MOU management
- mobility tracking
- scholarship/event management
- reporting workflows
- public information communication
- evidence collection and organizational knowledge management

Developed for:

University of Phayao  
International Relations Office  
Education Services Division

---

# 2. Project Vision

Develop a low-cost operational ecosystem that:

- reduces repetitive work
- centralizes information
- improves reporting workflows
- supports public communication
- improves accessibility of operational data
- supports long-term organizational knowledge retention
- supports professional development and future R2R work

The project aims to combine:

- real operational understanding
- low-cost technology
- AI-assisted development
- maintainable workflow design

into a practical system that can evolve gradually over time.

# 3. Initial Pain Points

## 3.1 Information Fragmentation

Before development:

- information existed across many Google Sheets
- files were scattered across personal folders
- workflows depended heavily on manual searching
- operational knowledge depended on memory
- evidence collection for reports was difficult

Observed impact:

- wasted time
- duplicated work
- inconsistent reporting
- difficulty tracking historical information
- operational stress during reporting periods

---

## 3.2 Manual Operational Workflows

Several workflows were heavily manual:

- scholarship announcements
- MOU tracking
- mobility statistics
- reporting
- annual summaries
- evidence collection
- public communication updates

Observed impact:

- high workload burden
- repeated formatting work
- duplicated data entry
- risk of inconsistency
- delays in information updates

---

## 3.3 Lack of Centralized Public Information

Public information lacked:

- centralized access
- searchable structure
- mobile-friendly display
- unified visual presentation
- real-time update capability

Affected areas included:

- scholarships
- international activities
- mobility opportunities
- announcements

## 3.4 Difficulty Collecting Professional Evidence

Evidence for:

- annual evaluations

The system is designed from:

# 6. Architecture Evolution Notes

## Initial Architecture

Characteristics:

- isolated pages
- inline logic
- duplicated utility functions
- page-specific CSS
- rapid prototyping

Advantages:

- fast experimentation
- low complexity initially

Disadvantages:

- duplication increased rapidly
- maintainability problems emerged
- inconsistent UI patterns appeared

---

## Current Architecture Direction

Target architecture:

Google Sheets (Data Layer)
↓
Apps Script APIs (Logic Layer)
↓
Shared Utilities + Shared Theme
↓
Reusable Components
↓
Admin + Public Interfaces
↓
GitHub Workflow
↓
Scalable Operational Ecosystem

Current priorities:

- utility centralization
- theme stabilization
- safe migrations
- reducing duplicated logic
- improving maintainability

# 7. AI-Assisted Development Reflections

## Observed Benefits

AI significantly helped with:

- reducing development time
- accelerating experimentation
- explaining unfamiliar concepts
- Git/GitHub adoption
- architecture planning
- debugging workflows
- documentation writing
- migration planning

---

## Important Lessons

AI works best when:

- operational goals are clear
- scope is controlled
- workflows are understood first
- architecture decisions are documented
- migrations are incremental

AI performs poorly when:

- goals are unclear
- scope changes constantly
- unnecessary rewrites are attempted
- architecture context is missing

---

## Development Insight

The project demonstrates that:

> operational staff with strong workflow understanding can build meaningful systems using AI-assisted development, even without traditional software engineering backgrounds.

# 11. Personal Reflection Notes

## Systems Thinking Development

Over time, development shifted from:

- isolated page editing

toward:

- maintainable systems thinking
- migration planning
- architecture awareness
- modular development
- operational ecosystem design

---

## Lessons About Development

Important realization:

> understanding workflows and operational pain points is often more valuable than knowing advanced syntax.

---

## Lessons About AI

AI is most effective when combined with:

- clear operational goals
- structured thinking
- documentation
- incremental workflows
- practical constraints

---

## Lessons About Scope

Large systems become unstable when:

- optimization happens too early
- architecture is rewritten repeatedly
- features expand faster than stabilization

Important principle learned:

> stable systems evolve gradually.
